03 / Qualification gate

Do not sell a name. Sell a proven deliverable.

1. Validate the offer

Document scope, target user, exclusions, prerequisites, real demo, delivery method, support and pricing basis.

2. Prove delivery

Run a representative test, retain the result and failure cases, and have an independent reviewer check the evidence.

3. Approve outreach

Verify the organisation and official contact source, personalise the message, check sending rules, and record approval.

4. Record outcomes

Track sent, delivered, bounced, replied, demo booked, proposal, won/lost and paid only from real provider or order records.

5. Protect trust

Use least-privilege access, keep secrets out of browser code, honour opt-outs, minimise personal data and preserve audit records.

6. Expand carefully

Increase outreach only when replies, delivery capacity and customer outcomes justify the next batch.

04 / Contact & next integration

Connect the real service layer

For this centre to become an operational sales system, it needs a server-side lead database, authenticated access, role-based approval, a compliant email provider, durable audit events, order intake and—if required—a payment provider. Secrets must remain server-side. The current page is intentionally transparent about these missing integrations.

  • Lead status: identified → qualification pending → qualified → draft ready → approval required → sent → replied → demo booked → proposal → won/lost.
  • Sales metrics must be sourced from real records and display their time window.
  • Revenue must remain unreported until a paid order or confirmed payment is recorded.
  • Use a verified public contact address before publishing a direct contact link.
